## Yoga and Movement {.wp-block-heading}

I began my personal journey with asana practice in 2005, seeking a compliment to an intense martial arts practice and an antidote to the accumulated injury and imbalance that resulted. I continued with intensive periods of study and practice in Varanasi, India in 2012, and in Rajpur in 2017. I completed an Intensive teacher training course with [Samyak Yoga][1] in Mysore in 2017.

While training in Plum Village in 2015-16 a good friend on the path introduced me to the work of [Donna Farhi][2], author of a number of exceptional books on the practice and teaching of Hatha Yoga. I was lucky enough to attend an intensive with Donna in Melbourne 2018, and she continues to be my touchstone for healthy asana practice and teaching technique.
