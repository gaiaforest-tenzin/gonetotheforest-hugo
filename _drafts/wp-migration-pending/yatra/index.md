## Yatra {.wp-block-post-title}

The Sanskrit for &#8220;Journey&#8221;, Yatra has also come to mean a pilgrimage, or sacred journey. Since childhood I have enjoyed bushwalking, but during my first experience of the Nepal Himalaya in 2012, I feel in love with walking as a spiritual practice. I have since had the good fortune to practice walking as a practice in mindful presence on yatra in Nepal, India, Tibet, and Vietnam. Since 2020 I have also had the good fortune to collaborate with Ronny Hickel and the <a href="http://www.yatra.org.au" data-type="URL" data-id="www.yatra.org.au">www.yatra.org.au</a> family in the creation of opportunities for others to experience the special container that yatra offers, journeying in mindfulness through beautiful wild places in Australia.
