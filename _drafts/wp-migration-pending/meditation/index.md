## Meditation {.wp-block-post-title}

Direct links to useful teachings From Bhante Dhammadipa:

##### ŚamatHa: {.wp-block-heading}

Recordings from a course in Śamatha meditation based on the śravakabhumi of the Yogacarabhumiśastra offered by Bhante Dhammadipa at the International Buddhist Academy in Kathmandu, Nepal, 2018.



##### Vipassana: {.wp-block-heading}

Recordings from a retreat on Four Elements meditation offered by Bhante Dhammadipa at the deer park Instistute in Bir, Himachal Pradesh, India, 2017



##### Kumarajiva&#8217;s meditation Manual: {.wp-block-heading}

Recordings from a series of lectures by Ven. Dhammadipa, starting from 3rd Feb 2021, organized by KJ Somaiya Institute of Dharma Studies, Somaiya Vidyavihar University in Mumbai. These teachings are based on one of the three meditation manuals ascribed to Kumarajiva in Chinese catalogues: the Chanfa yaojie, which Ven. Dhammadipa has translated into English. The special features of the text are the incorporation of bodhisattva ideas into classical Buddhist meditation. Bhante uses selections from the text to explicate the meditation method in comparative light, drawing extensively upon his study of the Abhidharmakosa and his long years of study and practice guided by his teachers of the forest tradition in Sri Lanka and Burma
