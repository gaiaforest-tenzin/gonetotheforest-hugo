## Myth and Memory {.wp-block-post-title}

&#8220;_And I come to the fields and spacious palaces of my memory, where are the treasures of innumerable images, brought into it from things of all sorts perceived by the senses_&#8220;. St Augustine, _Confessions_, Book X

An oral tradition for 2.5 thousand years, Buddhism relies much on memory. A few years ago I stumbled on Lynne Kelly&#8217;s fascinating book: [The Memory Code][1]. I&#8217;m posting here resources relating to theory and practice of remembering. See also this excellent article summarising Lynne Kelly&#8217;s thesis [here in Cosmos magazine][2]
