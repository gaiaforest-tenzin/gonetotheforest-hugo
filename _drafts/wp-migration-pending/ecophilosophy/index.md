## Sacred Ecology {.wp-block-post-title}

I am currently involved with a project to develop online access to Deep Ecology workshops. Inspired by the Ecophilosophy of Arne Naes and others, developed by John Seed, Joanna Macy and others, these workshops consist of a collection of group process aimed at addressing the ecological crisis we face by healing our perceived separation from the natural world. In response to the global environmental crisis Satish Kumar also invites us to develop a &#8220;Sacred Ecology&#8221;.

The website is still under construction, but can be found at <https://www.deepecology.org.au/>
