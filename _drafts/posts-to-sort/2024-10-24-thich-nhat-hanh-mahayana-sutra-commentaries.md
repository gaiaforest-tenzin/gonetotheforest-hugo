One of the great gifts I feel that I receive from Thich Nhat Hanh is a revival of my interest in teachings from the <a href="https://www.gaiaforest.org/tenzin/2023/01/11/sutta-central-too-clever/" data-type="post" data-id="1131">Pali canon</a> &#8211; exemplified in e.g. &#8220;Old Path White Clouds&#8221; &#8211; one of my favourite books from Thay.

3: Trimisika and Vimsika &#8211; Vasubandhu: 

T &#8220;<a href="https://www.gaiaforest.org/tenzin/2024/10/24/book-understanding-our-mind-thich-nhat-hanh/" data-type="post" data-id="2447">Understanding our Mind</a>&#8221; &#8211; Thich Nhat Hanh: is a commentary and response to the Thirty and Twenty verses of Vasubhandu &#8211; dealing with the nature of the Alayavijñana &#8211; Store Consciousness &#8211; cornerstone theory of the &#8220;mind-only&#8221; school and hence foundational texts for Yogacara Buddhism in general.
