Thank you for considering a donation. As a monastic, I endeavour to continue the ancient principle that places the Dharma beyond price. As such I offer whatever teachings and practices I have had the good fortune to receive from my teachers, and have understood to be helpful, completely free of charge.  If you find it beneficial also, please consider making an offering.  Any amount is much appreciated, and accepted in a spirit of "no giver, no receiver".  Small repeated offerings are even more helpful.  The generosity of the community is my only support for continued study and practice.
