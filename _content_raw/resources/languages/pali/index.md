Links and resources for journeying into the Pāli textual tradition:

#### Links

- [Sutta Central](https://suttacentral.net): online searchable repository of Pāli texts. If you know the code, e.g. [mn10](https://suttacentral.net/search?query=mn10), then you can go straight to the text you want, and even read it [in Pali and English side by side](https://suttacentral.net/mn10/en/sujato?layout=sidebyside&reference=none&notes=asterisk&highlight=false&script=latin), which is nice.
- With this integration of Voice with Sutta Central, you can listen to Pali texts recited by a robot: <https://voice.suttacentral.net>
- [Access to Insight](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/index.html) remains a favourite: a simple and accessible resource focused on English translations by trusted scholars (many of the same translations form the core at Sutta Central).
- Publications by Bhikkhu Anālayo from the Barre Center for Buddhist Studies: <https://www.buddhistinquiry.org/resources/offerings-analayo-old/publications/>
