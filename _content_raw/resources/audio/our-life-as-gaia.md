A guided meditation exploring "our life as Gaia".  This meditation was prepared as part of a 6 week online course in Deep Ecology.  It is inspired by the "Cosmic Spiral" process, often presented as part of a "Council of All Beings" workshop process.  This is a n original version re-written by me, aiming to shift the register into that of an embodied meditation practice, grounded in the four elements, as opposed to a ceremony relating facts and figures.
