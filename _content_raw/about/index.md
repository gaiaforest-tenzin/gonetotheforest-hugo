Welcome. This is the personal website of Ven. Ngawang Tenzin (Dr. Alex Anderson). Here I share information about [upcoming and past retreats, talks and other events](/news/events/).  You will also find a small and searchable library of [resources](/resources/), such as [audio](/resources/audio/) and [video](/resources/video/) recordings, [links](/resources/links/), [suggested readings](/resources/suggested-reading/), and downloadable [text resources](/resources/text/) (chanting, glossaries etc.).  Here you will also find a summary of my journey so far, my practice and study interests, and a way to [contact me](/contact/).  For information about our project to build space for mindfulness, meditation and nature connection in the mid-north coast hinterland of NSW, Australia, please visit the [gaia forest website]9https://www.gaiaforest.org
### A note about the site title:

"Gone to the forest" is a translation of the Pali "*araññagato*".  This word appears in the introduction to the the [*satipatthana* *sutta*](https://suttacentral.net/mn10/en/sujato?layout=sidebyside&reference=none&notes=asterisk&highlight=false&script=latin), in the phrase "araññagato vā, rukkhamūlagato vā, suññāgāragato vā"  instructions related to finding a suitable place for the cultivation of mindfulness. It has also been my life-long tendency.

For a little more about my journey so far, please visit the [Bio page](/bio/), or for a more detailed [CV](/cv/).
