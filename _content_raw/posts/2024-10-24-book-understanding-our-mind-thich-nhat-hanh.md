After &#8220;Old Path white Clouds&#8221; &#8211; This is possibly my favourite book of Thays. I feel through these versus a connection with <a href="https://www.gaiaforest.org/tenzin/2024/10/24/vasubhandu/" data-type="post" data-id="2443">Vasubandhu</a>, 21st patriarch of Chan, to who&#8217;s Thirty and Twenty verses this book form a sort of commentary and reply from one of the 21st Century&#8217;s foremost yogacara practitioners and philosophers.

This book has traveled with me on some form for several years &#8211; a sort of touchstone as to the true nature of mind, and a revealing of the store consciousness &#8211; (Sk: &#8220;alayavijñana&#8221;) as both source of all our seeds (Sk &#8220;bhija&#8221;), but also the possibility of &#8220;transformation at the base&#8221;.
