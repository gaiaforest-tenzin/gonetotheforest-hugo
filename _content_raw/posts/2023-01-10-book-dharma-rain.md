  Kaza, Stephanie, and Kenneth Kraft, eds. <em>Dharma Rain: Sources of Buddhist Environmentalism</em>. Boston and London: Shambhala, 2000.
</p>

An extensive collection of materials that the editors consider useful resources for the formulation and support of an environmentally oriented Buddhism. Contents are described as “classic Buddhist texts, modern commentaries, resources for ecologically oriented spiritual practice and guidelines for action”
