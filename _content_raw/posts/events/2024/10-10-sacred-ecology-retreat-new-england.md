A four-day retreat (10–13 October) in Gondwanan forest on Gumbaynggirr and Thungutti Country, hosted by Gaia Forest and facilitated by Br. Tenzin, exploring Buddhist teachings within a deep ecology framework — yoga and qi-gong, sitting meditation, silent bushwalking and dharma talks, with periods of noble silence.
