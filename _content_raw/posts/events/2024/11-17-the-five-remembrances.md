We live in uncertain times — politics, climate, relationships can all change dramatically in a short time, seemingly without warning. Tasked with the challenge of accepting things as they are, and "living joyfully in each moment," practitioners can sometimes feel overwhelmed. This talk explored how to move through change with dignity, courage and integrity, while keeping an open heart to the suffering we experience around us and within us. The Five Remembrances served as a valuable contemplation to guide us to the heart of the matter, recalling our attention to what is most important.
