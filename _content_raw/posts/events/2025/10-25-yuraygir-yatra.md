A nine-day walking pilgrimage (25 October – 2 November), co-facilitated with Emma Pittaway, from Lake Arragan through Sandon Head, Illaroo, Boorkoom and Station Creek, ending at Yarrawarra Aboriginal Cultural Centre.  Highlights included the beautiful and heartfelt Welcome to Country from Yaegl elder Aunty Glenda Kyle, Humpback whales seen from the beach every day,  and the wildness of the Wooli to Station Creek section.  We experienced some rain and windy weather,  but also idyllic days of sunshine, beach swims, and rich rest days with silent sits and  walking.  8 days of walking immersed in the stunning nature of the Yuraygir Coast have a powerful healing and grounding effect, and we concluded with a closing Gaia meditation on the last night, linking four elements  embodied meditation practice with the cosmic spiral process from deep ecology.  Stay tuned for more events like this in future.
