Br. Tenzin represented [yatra.org.au](https://yatra.org.au/) at this forum hosted by Outdoor Health Australia, the peak body supporting health, healing and resilience through outdoor and nature-based practice. Alongside sessions on indigenous youth work, therapeutic surfing, equine therapy and outdoor education, he facilitated a "sacred journey" workshop condensing an 8-day yatra retreat into one hour — mindful movement, breathwork, and a silent beach walk.
