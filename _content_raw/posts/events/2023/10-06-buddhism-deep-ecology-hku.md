A lecture exploring how Buddhist concepts — particularly Yogācāra doctrine and interdependent co-arising — connect with Deep Ecology philosophy, and how "ecological identity" can help address environmental crises and community wellbeing, drawing on Satyagraha movement ethics, Buddhist thought, and Gaia Theory. Free, all welcome.
