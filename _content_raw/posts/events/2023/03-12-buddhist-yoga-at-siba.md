A seven-day retreat exploring śamatha (calm abiding) practice through the lens of Mahayana traditions, drawing on the Saṃdhinirmocana Sūtra and the Awakening of Faith in Mahāyāna, examining the non-dual nature of mind across Zen and Tibetan Vajrayāna approaches. With optional yoga/Qi Gong, silent meditation, Dharma talks, and individual consultations. Taught by Bhante Dhammadipa, co-facilitated by Br. Tenzin (Dr. Alex Anderson). Offered on a dāna basis; registration fee covered venue, food, and logistics only.
