A seven-day immersive retreat exploring the theory and practice of the Four Immeasurable Minds, with Loving Kindness as the foundation alongside Compassion, Joy, and Equanimity — silent and guided meditation, Dharma talks, yoga/Qi Gong, and walking practices in noble silence. Taught by Bhante Dhammadipa, assisted by Br. Tenzin (Dr. Alex Anderson). Offered on a dāna basis.
