At Gaia Forest we host a weekly online Dharma discussion group exploring the intersection of the Buddha Dharma and Deep Ecology, as well as silent sits. Both are open to practitioners from all traditions. For the Dharma discussions we alternate between exploration of two texts: the Anapanasati Sutta and the Diamond Sutra. Please [get in touch](/contact/) if you are interested to join.
