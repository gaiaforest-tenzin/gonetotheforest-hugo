In this talk for the Indonesian Buddhist Society, we revisit the theme of [Silk Road Dharma](/posts/events/2026/11-03-silk-road-dharma-buddhist-library/)  — this time noticing the often-overlooked maritime route.  We meet the Tang-dynasty monk Yijing, who travelled by sea through the Sunda and Malacca Straits in the seventh century and found the Buddhist kingdom of Śrīvijaya to be such an important centre of learning that he recommended fellow Chinese monks study there before continuing on to Nālandā. From there we returned to *mettā* (loving-kindness) as a case study in translation — including how *karuṇā* (compassion) and *mettā* itself (*cinta kasih* in Indonesian) carry into our daily life — before closing with a guided *mettā* meditation.
