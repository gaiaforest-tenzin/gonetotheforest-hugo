In this talk for the Mettā Centre community in Parramatta we explored the cliché that meditation means "emptying your mind" — We began with the etymology of *jhāna* itself (which gives us *dhyāna*, *Chán* and *Zen*) meaning "to think," and then noted the Buddha's "I"-propositions to be avoided (MN 2), the subject/object split in Descartes and in contemporary neuroscience, and why we might do well to be wary of AI as a source of "wisdom" - it being based on "large language models" — with no capacity to feel. We closed with a guided sit and some excellent questions.
