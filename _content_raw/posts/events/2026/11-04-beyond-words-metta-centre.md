A talk for the Metta Centre taking apart the cliché that skilled meditation means "emptying your mind" — starting from the etymology of *jhāna* itself (which gives us *dhyāna*, *Chán* and *Zen*) meaning "to think," through the Buddha's "I"-propositions in MN 2, the subject/object split in Descartes and in contemporary neuroscience, and why a large language model — all pattern-matching, no capacity to feel — shows exactly what's missing from a mind with no path laid on top of its own conditioning. Closed with a guided sit and some excellent questions from the room and from Zoom.
