We host a weekly Sunday morning sit, 9–10:30am at the beautiful Never Never Mind community space at 16 Cudgery St, Dorrigo. The meet-ups consist of a 45 minute silent or guided sit, followed by a check-in and Dharma discussion. The group is open to all traditions, Buddhist and non-Buddhist, and all experience levels. Participation is by donation. Please [Get in touch](/contact/) if you have any questions or would like to be added to the list, otherwise simply turn up on Sunday a little before 9am.
