A 2-day immersive retreat led by Ven. Ngawang Tenzin (Dr. Alex Anderson), hosted by KFBG. Included mindful forest walks, meditation at Kwun Yam Shan, and gentle qigong with body awareness practices — centred on returning to the present moment through breathing, sensing, and reconnecting with nature. A follow-up to a well-received evening retreat the previous year.
