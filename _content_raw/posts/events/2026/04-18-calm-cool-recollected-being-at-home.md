Session 2 of a mini-retreat series (9:00am–4:30pm), exploring Vedananupassanā — the second foundation of mindfulness — examining how to work with pleasant and unpleasant feelings, through engaged practices of breath awareness, mindfulness in daily life, and metta (loving kindness). Led by Ven. Ngawang Tenzin (Dr. Alex Anderson). Free, with donations to the teacher welcome. In-person only.
