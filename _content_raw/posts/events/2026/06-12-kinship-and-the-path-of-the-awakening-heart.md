A weekend retreat (12–14 June) celebrating some of the theory and practice of awakening (*bodhi*) in the Buddhist tradition — mindfulness in daily life, and *kinship*, or loving kindness, drawing on practices from the Plum Village tradition. Held as part of Bodhi Farm's 50th Anniversary year; Thich Nhat Hanh himself visited the community in 1985. Offered as a series of optional activities, accessible to meditators and non-meditators alike, with particular attention to themes of community life — communication and connection — and to young people meeting the ecological crisis, and mental health and wellbeing in a world of social media.

## Follow-up

The retreat followed this program:

### Friday 12 June

- **Afternoon** — Arrival, with a possible visit to Dharmananda
- **5:30pm** — Early dinner
- **6:30pm** — Short introductory talk: "Bodhisattva — on the path of awakening"
- **7:30pm** — A short silent sit, to conclude the evening

### Saturday 13 June

- **6:30am** — Guided sitting meditation ("stopping and looking deeply")
- **7:15am** — Mindful movement practice
- **8:00am** — Breakfast
- **9:00am** — Community working bee (garden, where possible)
- **12:00pm** — Lunch
- **2:00pm** — Dharma talk: "Kinship and Eco-Dharma"
- **3:00pm** — Guided sitting meditation on kinship (*mettā*)
- **3:30pm** — Afternoon tea
- **4:00pm** — Guided meditation, "Our Life as Gaia" — a total relaxation practice with a Universe Story narrative
- **5:00pm** — Conclusion

### Sunday 14 June

- **8:00am** — Guided sitting meditation on kinship (*mettā*)
- **9:00am** — Breakfast
- **10:00am** — Concluding talk, with questions and answers
- **11:15am** — Optional sitting meditation
- **12:00pm** — Lunch
