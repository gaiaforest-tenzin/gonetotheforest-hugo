A Dharma talk on śamatha (calm abiding) and mettā bhāvanā (loving-kindness meditation), given at Vajra Ling Gompa, Rocky River, as part of a one-day practice session. Explores why mettā is traditionally offered as a bridge between calm-abiding and insight practice, the step-by-step structure of the classical mettā bhāvanā sequence (auxiliary being, self, dear one, neutral being, difficult being), and why "kinship" may be a more useful working translation of mettā than "loving-kindness."
