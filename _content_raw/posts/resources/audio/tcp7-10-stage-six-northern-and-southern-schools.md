A talk comparing northern (Mahayana) and southern (Theravada) approaches to śamatha-vipaśyanā (calm abiding and insight), and introducing the sixth stage of śamatha, śamayati (pacifying), followed by a self-guided silent sit. Explores emptiness, non-self, and dwelling happily in the present moment.
