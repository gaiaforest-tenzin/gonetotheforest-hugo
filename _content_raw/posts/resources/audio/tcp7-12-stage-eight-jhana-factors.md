A talk on the eighth stage of śamatha (calm abiding), ekotīkaraṇa (single-pointedness), and its correspondence to the jhāna factors of the first absorption, followed by a full guided breath and mettā-spreading meditation. Includes the story behind Thich Nhat Hanh's dharma name ("one practice") and Q&A on subtle-breath technique.
