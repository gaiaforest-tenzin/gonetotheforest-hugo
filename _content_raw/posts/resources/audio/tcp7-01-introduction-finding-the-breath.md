A short Buddhist meditation talk on finding the breath, followed by a full guided meditation — the opening session of a 15-part śamatha-vipaśyanā (calm abiding and insight) series recorded live at the International Buddhist Academy, Kathmandu. Ven. Ngawang Tenzin introduces the nine stages of śamatha through the classic monkey-mind and elephant imagery before leading the guided breath-awareness sit. A good entry point if you're new to Buddhist meditation or mindfulness of breathing.
