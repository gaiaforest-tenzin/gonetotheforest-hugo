A short talk on the four establishments of mindfulness (satipaṭṭhāna) and the first stage of śamatha (calm abiding) training, sthāpayati — simply placing the mind on the breath — followed by a 25-minute self-guided silent meditation. Covers the three types of wisdom and the Buddha's mindfulness-of-breathing instructions.
