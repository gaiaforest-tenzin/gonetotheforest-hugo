A short talk introducing mettā bhāvanā (loving-kindness meditation), one of the four brahmavihāra, and its role as both foundation for and protection during vipaśyanā (insight) practice, followed by a full guided loving-kindness meditation. Includes the story of the Buddha's Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta.
