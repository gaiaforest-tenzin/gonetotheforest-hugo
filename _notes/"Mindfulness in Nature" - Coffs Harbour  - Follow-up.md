# Mindfulness in Nature  Coffs Harbour 
Short program as part of the [Our Living Coast](https://www.ourlivingcoast.com.au/Home) offerings - organised by Lilli Nicholson.

## Summary
This was a two-hour session held in on Gumbaynggirr country in the beautiful setting of Coffs Harbour Botanic Gardens.  Participants enjoyed a 35 minute movement practice based on principles of [[qi-gong]] and yoga (link to video coming soon) - these exercises are a low-impact and accessible way to begin to develop sensitivity to subtle sensations in the body - important for embodied mindfulness practice including [[sitting meditation]] and [[mindfulness of breathing]].

This was followed by a brief introduction to mindful walking or [[walking meditation]], including some reflections and practices offered in the [[Plum Village]] tradition.  We then enjoyed a slow and mindful 45 minute walk through some of the beautiful natural forest and plantings in the gardens, concluding with a group sharing circle.

## Follow-up resources

### "The Satipathana attitude"
Guidance during the session referred to a few key practices in mindfulness and meditation practices, especially as they are shared in the Buddhist Tradition; including the sutra on the "Four Establishments of Mindfulness", or [Satipathana Sutta].  Of four domains introduced therein,  "Mindfulness of the Body" is the first.  A key instruction was around a "satipathana attitude" consisting of keeness, clear understanding, ,mindfulness, and not grasping or pushing away any aspect of our experience.
